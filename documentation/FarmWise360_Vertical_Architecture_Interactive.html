STAGE 1
Dataset Preparation & Ingestion
Agricultural Knowledge Curation Pipeline
What Happens in this Stage
Extracts, cleans, and standardizes authoritative agricultural documents (ICAR research papers, TNAU advisories) instead of using unverified noisy CSV datasets.
Technical Working Mechanism
1. OCR Noise Removal: Filters broken text, header lines, and page numbers from PDFs.
2. Semantic Chunking: Divides text into self-contained advice blocks tagged with crop and disease labels.
3. BPE Tokenizer: 4,000-vocabulary model preserving scientific chemical names as single tokens.
